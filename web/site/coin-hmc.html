HackMe Coin (HMC)

Utility — why the token exists

Mining

Pool work that finds vulnerabilities in real code settles to on-chain HMC.

Security scans

Customers escrow HMC for Dig/Hunt campaigns — demand beyond speculation.

Network fees

Transfers pay a small HMC fee; from block 550,000 every paid order also burns 5% of its escrow value for real.

Allocation & vesting

Everything beyond the public 50,000 HMC genesis treasury enters through block emission and paid orders. No team pre-mine, no airdrops.
