Community rules of engagement

  • Never share admin tokens, private keys, or raw wallet seeds in public chats.
  • Download binaries only from hackme.tech/downloads.html and verify SHA256.
  • Trust github.com/jokeez/hackme for source — forks may impersonate; check the official org.
  • For bug reports, include endpoint, status code, and reproducible steps.
  • Security disclosures: contact privately via support@hackme.tech before public discussion.
