Security rewards

Bug bounty & UX reports (HMC)

Private reports welcome. We pay small on-chain HMC grants after verified fixes — not a token sale, not guaranteed income. Amounts are guidelines; the operator decides each case.

Reward tiers (guideline)

Severity Examples Typical HMC
Informational Docs typo, duplicate known issue 0 (thanks)
Low — UI/UX Broken layout, wrong link on official site 1 – 5
Low — functional Minor API/validation issue, no exploit 2 – 10
Medium Auth leak, DoS with PoC, coordinator issue without drain 10 – 40
High Settlement bug with fund impact, sandbox escape PoC 40 – 100
Critical Drain treasury/pool accounting, consensus break 100 – 200

Monthly program budget (discretionary): ~200 HMC total across payouts until reviewed.

Rules (short)

  • Private first — use Contacts; do not post working exploits on forums or GitHub issues.
  • Official builds only — verify SHA256 from Downloads.
  • One reward per root cause — duplicates may be declined or split.
  • Payment after fix — we send HMC to your HMC-… address when resolved.
  • Out of scope: phishing clones, miner payout disputes, scanner noise without impact.

In scope

Production stack at hackme.tech: node, coordinator, worker, nginx, WASM PoH path, hybrid signer, settlement tooling.
