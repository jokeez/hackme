Reward tiers

Guidelines per accepted root cause — operator decides each case.

Informational 0 HMC

Docs typo, duplicate known issue — thanks / credit only.

Low · UI/UX 1 – 5

Broken layout, wrong link on the official site.

Low · functional 2 – 10

Minor API/validation gap, no exploit path.

Medium 10 – 40

Auth leak, DoS with PoC, coordinator issue without drain.

High 40 – 100

Settlement bug with fund impact, sandbox escape PoC.

Critical 100 – 200

Treasury/pool drain, consensus break.

Rules (short)

  • Private first — use Contacts; do not post working exploits publicly.
  • Official builds only — verify SHA256 from Downloads.
  • One reward per root cause — duplicates may be declined or split.
  • Payment after fix — HMC to your HMC-… address when resolved.
  • Public credit optional — hall listing needs your OK (GitHub or nick).

In scope

Production stack at hackme.tech: node, coordinator, worker, nginx, WASM PoH path, hybrid signer, settlement tooling.

  • Out of scope: phishing clones, miner payout disputes, scanner noise, the published pool.miner.token, pool-direct cleartext HTTP for miners, documented public wallet aggregates.
