C network packet parser

Customer pilot · Shannon Bautista · published with consent · 2026-07-07

Scan package Promo #1 of 5

What we did: Customer sent target.c (LLVMFuzzer-style entry → test_fuzz_target) plus two binary seeds. We wrapped the parser in a WASM guard, compiled C→WASM locally, and ran Scan (64 runs · byte corpus · customer seeds).

Results:

  • seed1.bin — HACK\x00 (valid header, zero-length payload) → clean
  • seed2.bin — HACK\x03BUG (customer’s intentional crash test) → guard hit on every other corpus cycle (32/64 runs)

Honest scope: This is a WASM sandbox Scan, not a native audit or CVE claim. seed2 was supplied by the customer as a known crash repro — we report guard signals, not “32 production vulnerabilities”.
