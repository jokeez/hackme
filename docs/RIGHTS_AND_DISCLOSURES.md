## 5. Risk disclosure (summary)

Participation in mining, running nodes, or holding ecosystem tokens involves:

1. **No guaranteed profitability** — rewards depend on network conditions, competition, and protocol economics.
2. **Availability risk** — outages, provider failures, or maintenance may interrupt services.
3. **Software risk** — complex distributed systems may contain undiscovered defects.
4. **Security risk** — compromised credentials or misconfiguration can cause loss.
5. **Regulatory risk** — obligations vary by jurisdiction; users must assess local compliance.
6. **Multi-coin complexity** — HMC, SUP, and HMS have different emission and listing status.

## 7. Operator vs community fork

**Official operator moat** is operational leadership, not hidden binaries:

- Canonical hub at hackme.tech
- Published settlement timers and operator gates
- Community trust, checksums, and transparent treasury disclosure

A **legitimate fork** renames the project, keeps AGPL attribution, and runs on its own domain. An **abuse** copies branding to phish miners — report to hosts and document official URLs.
